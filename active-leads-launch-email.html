03 · The language

What each stage actually means.

Stage is the field the whole page keys off. It drives the four metrics, the filter counts and every row in your export.

  New Opportunity

Landed, not yet worked. Sitting in the queue for a sales manager.

  Outreach

Contact made, chasing a reply. Dates and rate still unknown.

  Qualified

The requirement is real and it fits the hotel. A proposal is worth building.

  Interested HOT

Proposal is out and the planner is engaged. This is where your attention pays.

  Contracting HOT

Terms agreed, contract moving between the parties. A signature is all that is missing.

  Tentative

Signed and committed on paper, block not yet confirmed in the system.

  Confirmed BOOKED

Rooms blocked, booking stands. Committed base revenue until the guests check in.

  Cancelled

Dead, from any stage. Removed from Active Leads, Hot Leads and Pipeline Value at once.
